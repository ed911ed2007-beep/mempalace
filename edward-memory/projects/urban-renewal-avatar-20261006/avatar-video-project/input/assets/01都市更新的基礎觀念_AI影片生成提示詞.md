# 01 都市更新的基礎觀念｜AI 影片生成提示詞

## 目的
將《01都市更新的基礎觀念講稿》轉換為可用於 GENAPE / VideoApe 類 AI 影片生成平台的視覺提示詞，並與既有「土開 Avatar.mp4」結合，製作都市更新基礎觀念教學影片。

## 影片定位
- 類型：都市更新知識教學 / 房地產開發教育影片
- 主講角色：沿用 `土開 Avatar.mp4`
- 畫面比例：16:9
- 風格：專業、可信、現代、台灣都市更新情境
- 色調：自然日光、暖灰、白色、淺藍、建築專業視覺
- 使用方式：Avatar 負責口播，AI 生成畫面作為 B-roll、示意場景、背景與轉場
- 重要限制：避免生成可辨識商標、錯誤法條文字、亂碼中文字、虛構公文、錯誤政府 Logo
- 建議：涉及法規或條文時，用「抽象文件、圖表、會議、都市街景」呈現，不要求 AI 直接生成可讀法條

Create a photorealistic cinematic educational video about urban renewal in Taiwan.

The video should visually support a professional presenter Avatar explaining the basic concepts of urban renewal.

Use realistic Taiwan urban environments: older residential buildings, apartment blocks, narrow streets, construction areas, community meeting rooms, government-style planning environments, architectural models, renovation scenes, elevators being added to old buildings, exterior facade improvement, and modern replacement buildings.

Visual style:
professional real-estate documentary,
urban planning education,
clean corporate visual language,
natural daylight,
realistic architecture,
subtle cinematic camera movement,
slow dolly shots,
aerial urban views,
medium-wide composition,
high detail,
credible Taiwanese city context,
16:9.

Do not generate:
readable legal text,
fake government logos,
brand logos,
random Chinese characters,
watermarks,
English subtitles,
oversized text overlays,
unrealistic futuristic buildings,
disaster imagery,
overdramatic demolition scenes.
